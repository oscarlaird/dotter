\chapter{Byte-Pair Encoding}
\label{chap:bpe}

This chapter collects the material in this book that depends specifically on byte-pair
encoding (BPE). Section~\ref{sec:bpe-mechanics} contains the formal mechanics of stagewise
BPE tokenization and the proof of canonical concatenation. Section~\ref{sec:bpe-spines}
then gives an implementation-oriented view based on merge IDs, left and right spines,
and a fast boundary test for token-pair canonicality.
